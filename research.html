Research statement

From constraints to useful structure

Realistic quantum platforms are rarely clean collections of individually controlled qubits. They are open, interacting, and often accessible only through collective operations. I treat these limitations as part of the physics: a route to new encodings, observables, and thermodynamic protocols.

The unifying aim is to identify operational advantages that survive realistic control and noise—whether the task is storing information, diagnosing entanglement, extracting work, or charging a quantum battery.
